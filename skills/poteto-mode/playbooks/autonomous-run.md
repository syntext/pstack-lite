### Autonomous run

**You own the exit condition. Define done, then drive to it without stopping.** One goal needing sustained evidence-driven iterations belongs here. A program of dependent owners belongs to Orchestrate. This continuation policy wraps the matching retained engineering playbook; it does not replace its implementation, review, or verification steps.

1. State the exit condition as a checkable predicate before the first iteration. Use supplied requirements and acceptance criteria. A request to describe a plan is not permission to execute it. Keep planning inputs read-only unless completion-marker edits are explicitly requested.
2. Choose the matching engineering playbook and execute its steps. Discover actual runtime continuation capabilities. If an available event watcher or wake mechanism is established, record how it works. Otherwise continue while the session supports execution and wait for real child notifications. Do not claim a scheduled heartbeat, unattended wakeup, or restart recovery merely because you can sleep in a shell. When execution cannot continue, leave a durable resume point and disclose the limitation.
3. Each iteration makes the smallest change the evidence justifies, verifies it against the predicate, and commits if it advanced and commits are authorized. Remove only your unsuccessful experimental changes; never discard pre-existing work. Sequence verifiable units instead of batching checks at the end. Preserve the leaf playbook's review responsibilities and local-default finish.
4. Mid-run discoveries do not expand the supplied scope. Address authorized blockers through the matching workflow and return to the predicate. Report unrelated findings as follow-ups. Surface irreversible actions, genuine product or preference calls no experiment can settle, changed requirements, or a real dead end. Do not silently rewrite intent or acceptance criteria.
5. Checkpoint every iteration through **show-me-your-work**, recording what changed, the exact revision or dirty-state snapshot, evidence, and whether the predicate moved. Reuse Pause safely and Session pickup when a handoff is necessary. Bound failure retries as in Orchestrate's failure section; do not introduce its multi-unit store for a single goal.
6. Stop when the predicate is met and verified on the real artifact. A plateau is not success; pivot your approach on evidence. Surface a genuine dead end rather than spinning, and never relax the predicate to declare victory. On the operator's hold, stop dispatch and use actual cancellation controls where available; disclose any still-active child rather than promising it stopped.

**Reply:** the exit condition, iterations run, what changed, what experiments were removed, verification and final predicate state, remaining gaps, and trail or resume-note path. No push or PR unless explicitly requested.
